Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our legal services

How do I know if I need a lawyer?

If you're facing legal matters, disputes, or need contract review, consulting with an advocate can save you time and money. We offer free initial consultations to assess your situation.

What are your fees and billing structure?

We offer flexible billing options including hourly rates, fixed fees, and contingency arrangements depending on your case type. We'll discuss pricing during your consultation.

How long does a typical case take?

Case duration varies depending on complexity, court schedules, and dispute nature. We'll provide a realistic timeline after reviewing your specific situation.

Is attorney-client privilege confidential?

Absolutely. All communications between you and our advocates are strictly confidential and protected by attorney-client privilege, ensuring your information remains secure.

Do you handle cases outside Nairobi?

Yes, we can handle matters in other jurisdictions through partnerships and arrangements with other legal practitioners across Kenya.

Can I get a free consultation?

Yes! We offer a free initial consultation where we listen to your concerns and advise whether we can help. Contact us via phone, email, or WhatsApp to schedule.
